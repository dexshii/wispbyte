      About Wispbyte

      We’re four friends who like the strange corners of the map: crooked dungeons, quiet ghosts and creatures that look a little too pleased to see you.

      Wispbyte is our small studio for building those places as games on Roblox. We make the art, write the code and test every run together, one odd idea at a time.

      • ☾

        Strange worlds

        Moonlit ruins, cursed objects and places that don’t quite follow the rules.

      • ◈

        Curious creatures

        Hand-made creatures with personality, from tiny wisps to things with too many eyes.

      • ✦

        Made for Roblox

        Games built to play great with friends, right inside Roblox.
